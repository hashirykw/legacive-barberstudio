The story

AK's story

Fresh cut, fresh confidence. Feel the difference.

Legacive is AK's studio. He sharpened his craft behind the chair at Dynasty Barber Studio, then built a room of his own in Uxbridge: dark walls, hex lights, one chair and the time to get every cut right.

The studio

Built under hex light.

A blacked-out room, faceted 3D walls, a hexagon light ceiling and a ring light on every cut. Lighting this clean shows every line, so nothing leaves the chair unfinished.
