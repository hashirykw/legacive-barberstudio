    Good to know.

    Quick answers before you book. Anything else, the chat bubble or a text gets you an answer.

    Do you take walk-ins?

    No. Legacive is appointments only, so the chair is always ready at your time and you never sit in a queue.

    How do I book?

    Book instantly on Booksy, or send a request on the Book page and AK will text you to confirm the time.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Text the studio at (289) 988-5515 as early as you can so the slot can go to someone else.

    Can I bring a reference photo?

    Yes, please do. A photo is the fastest way to agree on length, fade height and shape.

    Do you cut curly hair?

    Yes. Curly tops, crops and textured cuts are a big part of the work. See the gallery for examples.

    Can I add a design to my cut?

    Any cut can get a design, hard part or neck design. It usually adds about $10 and 15 minutes.
